# AI-Era Security Awareness Requirements

Security awareness must now address AI-enabled threats and the safe use of AI tools.

## AI-Enabled Social Engineering

Employees should understand that phishing and fraud attempts may now be polished, personalized, and convincing.

Training should cover:

- AI-generated phishing emails
- Voice cloning
- Deepfake audio and video
- AI-assisted business email compromise
- Multi-channel social engineering

## Expected Behaviours

Employees should:

1. Verify unusual requests through a trusted second channel.
2. Avoid relying on voice, video, or writing style alone as proof of identity.
3. Escalate requests involving money, credentials, MFA codes, sensitive data, or approval bypasses.
4. Report suspicious activity even where there is no link or attachment.

## AI Tool Acceptable Use

Employees should understand:

- Which AI tools are approved
- What information must not be entered into public AI tools
- Why AI outputs require human review
- How to handle confidential, personal, customer, security, or regulated data

## High-Risk Requests

High-risk requests include:

- Bank detail changes
- Urgent payment instructions
- Password or MFA reset requests
- Requests to share confidential documents
- Requests to bypass normal approval workflows
- Executive instructions received through unusual channels
