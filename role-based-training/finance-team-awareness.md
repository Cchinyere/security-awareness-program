# Finance Team Security Awareness

## Purpose

Finance teams are high-risk targets for fraud, business email compromise, payment redirection, and supplier impersonation.

## Key Risks

- Invoice fraud
- Supplier bank detail changes
- Executive impersonation
- Urgent payment requests
- Business email compromise
- Social engineering by phone or email

## Required Behaviours

Finance staff should:

1. Verify payment changes using trusted contact details.
2. Challenge urgent or unusual payment requests.
3. Follow approval workflows consistently.
4. Escalate suspected fraud immediately.
5. Avoid relying only on email instructions for financial changes.

## AI-Era Considerations

Finance teams should be aware that attackers may use:

- AI-generated emails
- Voice cloning
- Deepfake audio or video
- Highly personalized fraud attempts

## Verification Protocol

Before processing high-risk payment changes:

1. Stop and assess the request.
2. Verify using a trusted contact method.
3. Obtain required approval.
4. Record verification steps.
5. Report suspicious pressure or unusual instructions.
