# Phishing Reporting Drill

## Purpose

To test whether employees can recognize and report suspicious messages quickly and correctly.

## Scope

This exercise may be used for:

- All staff
- Specific departments
- New starters
- High-risk teams

## Rules

The exercise must be:

- Approved before launch
- Ethical and proportionate
- Educational, not punitive
- Designed without public shaming
- Followed by learning feedback

## Metrics

Track:

- Reporting rate
- Time to first report
- Click rate
- Repeat susceptibility
- Quality of reports

## Follow-Up

After the drill:

1. Share learning points.
2. Thank employees who reported.
3. Avoid naming or shaming individuals.
4. Update awareness content based on results.
