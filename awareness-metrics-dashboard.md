# Awareness Metrics Dashboard

## Purpose

This Awareness Metrics Dashboard helps organizations measure the effectiveness of their security awareness program.

The purpose is not only to track whether employees completed training, but also to understand whether awareness activities are improving behaviour, reducing human-related cyber risk, and supporting a stronger reporting culture.

## Dashboard Objectives

The objectives of this dashboard are to:

1. Track participation in security awareness activities.
2. Measure improvement in reporting behaviour.
3. Identify high-risk teams or recurring awareness gaps.
4. Monitor phishing simulation results ethically and constructively.
5. Track AI-era awareness risks, including unsafe AI tool use and deepfake-related reporting.
6. Support leadership reporting and continuous improvement.
7. Provide evidence for audit, compliance, and governance reviews.

## Dashboard Audience

This dashboard may be used by:

- Security teams
- GRC teams
- Compliance teams
- HR or People teams
- IT leadership
- Executive sponsors
- Department managers
- Security awareness program owners

## 1. Participation Metrics

Participation metrics show whether employees and target groups are completing assigned awareness activities.

| Metric | Description | Suggested Target | Reporting Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core Training Completion Rate | Percentage of staff who completed annual core security awareness training | 95% or above | Monthly |
| New Starter Completion Rate | Percentage of new starters completing onboarding security training within required timeframe | 100% within 10 working days | Monthly |
| Role-Based Training Completion | Percentage of high-risk teams completing assigned role-based training | 90% or above | Quarterly |
| Executive Training Completion | Percentage of executives completing annual cyber risk briefing | 100% | Annually |
| Privileged User Training Completion | Percentage of privileged users completing required training | 100% | Quarterly |
| AI Acceptable Use Training Completion | Percentage of staff completing AI acceptable use training | 95% or above | Quarterly |
| Security Champion Attendance | Percentage of security champions attending scheduled briefings | 80% or above | Quarterly |

## 2. Behaviour Metrics

Behaviour metrics show whether employees are applying secure behaviours in practice.

| Metric | Description | Suggested Target | Reporting Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suspicious Email Reporting Rate | Percentage of users reporting suspicious emails or messages | Increase over time | Monthly |
| Time to First Report | Time between simulated phishing delivery and first employee report | Reduce over time | Per simulation |

## 4. Phishing Simulation Metrics

Phishing metrics should be used to improve learning, not to shame individuals.

| Metric | Description | Suggested Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting Rate | Percentage of users who reported the phishing simulation | Higher is better |
| Click Rate | Percentage of users who clicked the link | Lower is better |
| Submission Rate | Percentage of users who entered information, if safely simulated | Lower is better |
| Time to First Report | How quickly the first report was received | Faster is better |
| Repeat Susceptibility | Users or teams repeatedly interacting with simulations | May indicate need for support |
| Department Trends | Comparison of results across departments | Use carefully and constructively |
| Training Completion After Simulation | Completion of follow-up training after simulation | Higher is better |

## Phishing Metrics Governance

Phishing simulation data should be used ethically.

Do not:

- Publicly name employees who clicked.
- Use results to embarrass individuals.
- Treat one click as misconduct.
- Rank teams in a way that creates blame.
- Ignore reporting improvements.
- Focus only on click rates.

Do:

- Share aggregated results.
- Celebrate reporting.
- Provide supportive follow-up.
- Use results to improve controls and training.
- Track trends over time.
- Focus on learning and resilience.

## Status Rating Guide

Use a simple Red, Amber, Green rating.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | Target met or performance improving |
| Amber | Slightly below target or requires monitoring |
| Red | Below target or requires action |
| Grey | Data not available or not yet measured |
